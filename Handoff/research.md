# Satellite Demo — research entry point

The complete cited survey is preserved in [reference/research.md](reference/research.md): fourteen selected public repositories, selected source inspection in seven, and six award examples. External project names remain unchanged; they are not our placeholder name.

## What carries into the current build

- Reuse numerical, web, and visualization libraries; keep their attribution. Write the small, tested core and workflow specified in [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md).
- Learn candidate enumeration and result presentation from [SatGuard](https://github.com/cesabici-bit/satguard) and the existing [OrbitGuard](https://github.com/Nishant7p/OrbitGuard). Do not treat their approximate maneuver results as our verified outcomes.
- Learn frame and error handling from [OrbVeil](https://github.com/ncdrone/orbveil).
- Use [React Three Fiber](https://github.com/pmndrs/react-three-fiber) to render Three.js scenes within React. An optional model can use [Three.js GLTFLoader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFLoader.html).
- Do not spend the event adapting an entire platform, training a model, or rebuilding the demo around a repository merely because its README looks complete.

Prior winners show that the theme can compete; they do not prove our twenty-hour schedule or a novel invention. See the archive for exact distinctions between first place, national wins, placements, and nominations.

The current package overrides the archive's earlier optional-3D and component-adaptation decisions. No external repository was executed as part of this handoff update.
